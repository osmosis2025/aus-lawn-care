Agronomic & Technical Sources

Maintenance guidelines and turf sensitivities synthesized from Australian industry research and technical extension bodies:

  • Lawn Solutions Australia (LSA): Industry benchmarks for Sir Walter Buffalo, TifTuf, and Zoysia seasonal height and fertilisation.
  • Australian Turfgrass Management Research (ATRI/ASTMA): Pre-emergent timing and pest management protocols (Armyworm & Lawn Grub).
  • State Departments of Primary Industries (NSW DPI & QLD DAF): Agronomic research on soil temperature thresholds, weed cycles, and turf herbicide tolerance limits (e.g. Dicamba exclusions).
